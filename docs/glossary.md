# Glossary

Shared domain terms for every agent.

- Statuses: todo, in_progress, blocked, done. Priorities: low, medium, high, urgent.
- Overdue: due date passed and status ≠ done. Computed, never stored.
- Delayed: completed after its due date.
- Completion rate (period): completed in period ÷ due in period.
- On-time rate: completed on or before due ÷ all completed in period.
- Average delay: mean days late over delayed tasks.
- Leader: creates, assigns, edits and deletes team tasks; sees all team data and every member's stats.
- Member: sees and updates only tasks assigned to them; posts in team chat; sees only their own stats.
